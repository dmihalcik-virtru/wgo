# gh-105 — Sprint view

**Issue:** https://github.com/dmihalcik-virtru/wgo/issues/105
**Epic:** [gh-96](gh-96.md) · **Wave 3** · Needs gh-98, gh-99 and gh-100; uses gh-97

**Question it answers:** "What is the state of the tracked tickets in this
active sprint, and where does Jira disagree with their known linked PRs?"

## Summary

One view per active sprint: its goal verbatim, the time elapsed, tickets
assigned to configured people or unassigned, their known linked PR states,
and gh-99 mismatches. The view names that population throughout; it does not
present its ticket-status distribution as completion of the sprint goal.

## Requirements

1. An addon view `web/live-sprint.js`, registered as `sprint`. With several
   active sprints, a selector picks one (`sprint=` in the URL); the default is
   the sprint with the most tickets for the configured people.
2. Header:
   - sprint name, start and end dates;
   - a thin bar of elapsed time with "day 6 of 21";
   - the goal as plain text. When the goal is empty, the header says "no
     sprint goal";
   - "Tickets assigned to configured people or unassigned" and the number
     returned. If gh-98 marks the search stale, unknown or truncated, show
     that beside the count, including the source time and limit; never call
     a partial result the whole sprint.
3. Ticket-status line: story points in the fetched population as one
   horizontal bar divided into To Do, In Progress and Done. Each part is
   directly labelled with points and ticket count. Tickets with unknown
   status category are counted separately as "n unknown status";
   unestimated tickets as "n unestimated", not zero points. With no known
   points, show the ticket counts without a proportional bar. The bar's
   denominator is points on tickets with a known status category; any known
   points on unknown-status tickets are reported separately. The label says
   "status of fetched tickets", not "goal progress".
4. Ticket table, grouped by status category, then assignee:
   - key and summary (linked);
   - assignee, points, status;
   - known linked PRs as glyphs with refs: draft `◇`, open and awaiting review
     `○`, approved `✓`, merged `●`, closed `✕`. gh-99's move is on hover;
   - mismatch codes as short text with a `!` glyph; if gh-99 says a mismatch
     is unknown because a source is incomplete, show that uncertainty and its
     reason instead of a reassuring empty mismatch cell.

   Rows can be selected, and the selection is shared with every view.
5. Clicking a mismatch code applies `mismatch:<code>` (gh-100). Clicking the
   ticket-status bar's parts applies `status_category:<c>`.
6. The view respects the current query: tickets outside it are dimmed, not
   hidden, so the fetched sprint slice stays visible. A toggle hides them
   instead. Show "n of m tickets match this query". The header and status
   line always summarize all fetched tickets in the selected sprint, even
   when rows are dimmed or hidden by the query.
7. "Copy as Markdown" (gh-100) on this view requests
   `/api/extract?view=sprint&sprint=<id>&q=<query>` and produces a sprint
   summary: goal, dates, status-category points and ticket counts for the
   full fetched slice, mismatches with links, and a separate list of tickets
   matching the query. A ticket outside the query remains in the full-slice
   totals but not in the match list. The provenance footer names the
   configured-people-and-unassigned scope, returned count, truncation,
   selected sprint ID, active query and source freshness. `wgo dash --md
   --view sprint --sprint ID --query EXPR` calls the same renderer.
8. With no `[jira] board`, or `jira_sprints` or the selected sprint's
   `ticket_lookup` unknown/error, the view explains which is missing and the
   relevant remedy (for example `[jira] board = <id>` or `acli jira auth
   login`), instead of rendering an empty sprint. Stale ticket data remains
   visible with its timestamp.

## Out of scope

- Editing tickets or sprints.
- Burndown, velocity or forecasts.
- Board-wide sprint totals, goal-completion scoring, and sprints other than
  active ones. Future and recent-closed sprints require follow-up specs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The fixture sprint renders goal, elapsed time, the ticket-status bar with
      direct labels, and unestimated and unknown-status tickets counted
      separately. Its population label includes unassigned tickets and does
      not imply a board-wide goal-progress percentage.
- [ ] Each gh-99 mismatch in the fixture appears on its ticket and filters
      when clicked; unknown mismatch evidence appears as unknown.
- [ ] A query dims or hides nonmatching rows and changes the match count,
      while header/status totals remain for all fetched tickets. A truncated
      or stale search is labelled in the view and extract; unknown ticket
      coverage does not render as zero tickets.
- [ ] The Markdown sprint summary matches a golden file and is byte-for-byte
      identical from the CLI and `/api/extract` for the same snapshot,
      sprint ID and query.
- [ ] Missing configuration and unknown sprint data produce the explanatory
      state.
- [ ] Keyboard-only use and a colour-vision-deficiency simulation pass
      (screenshots in the PR, light and dark).
- [ ] `go test ./...` passes.
